#  HR Analytics Dashboard 

## 📌 Overview
The **HR Analytics Dashboard** is an interactive Tableau report built to analyze key Human Resource metrics such as attrition, job satisfaction, demographics, and workforce distribution.  
It empowers HR leaders and managers with actionable insights to monitor workforce trends, identify attrition risks, and develop data-driven retention strategies.

---

##  Business Objective
To monitor employee attrition patterns, analyze demographic factors affecting turnover, and assess job satisfaction trends across departments, roles, and age groups.

---

##  Key KPIs & Metrics Tracked

- **Total Employee Count**
- **Attrition Count & Attrition Rate (%)**
- **Active Employees**
- **Average Employee Age**
- **Attrition by Department**
- **Attrition by Age Group**
- **Attrition by Gender**
- **Education Field-wise Attrition**
- **Job Satisfaction by Role**
- **Employee Distribution by Age Group**

---

##  Visuals & Insights Provided

- **KPI Cards:** Total Employees, Attrition Count, Attrition Rate, Active Employees, Average Age
- **Pie Chart:** Attrition Distribution by Department
- **Bar Chart:** Education Field-wise Attrition
- **Histogram:** Employee Count by Age Group
- **Donut Charts:** Attrition by Age Group & Gender
- **Heatmap Table:** Job Satisfaction Breakdown by Role
- **Gender Split Visualization:** Attrition Analysis by Gender

---
### Key Business Insights

### 1️⃣ Overall Attrition Rate is High (20.63%)
- Out of **446 employees**, **92 have left**, resulting in a **20.63% attrition rate**.
- Highlights a significant retention challenge.

---

### 2️⃣ Departmental Attrition Breakdown
- **Sales Department:** Highest attrition (**56.12%** of total attrition).
- **R&D Department:** Accounts for **38.82%** of total attrition.
- **HR Department:** Lowest attrition at **5.06%**.

---

### 3️⃣ Age-wise Attrition Trends
- **25–34 Age Group:** Highest attrition (**39 employees left**, ~23.9% attrition rate).
- Older groups show decreasing attrition rates; **Over 55** shows no attrition.
- Young workforce at higher risk of turnover.

---

### 4️⃣ Job Satisfaction Patterns
- **Sales Executives** show the most low satisfaction scores.
- **Managerial roles** exhibit more balanced satisfaction scores.
- Indicates low job satisfaction may contribute to turnover, especially in Sales.

---

### 5️⃣ Education Field Insights
- Higher attrition in employees with **Marketing** and **Life Sciences** backgrounds.
- Lower attrition for those with **Medical** or **Technical Degrees**.
- Suggests education background impacts retention.

---

### 6️⃣ Gender Split in Attrition
- **Males account for 54% of total attrition**, while **females account for 38%**.
- Gender-wise attrition patterns align with department and role distributions.

---

### 7️⃣ Average Employee Age is 36.5 Years
- Indicates a mid-career workforce.
- Attrition predominantly affects the **25–44 age group**.

---
