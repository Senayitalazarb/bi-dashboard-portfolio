#  HR Analytics Dashboard 

## 📌 Overview
The **HR Analytics Dashboard** is an interactive Tableau report that analyzes key human resource metrics like attrition, job satisfaction, and employee demographics. It helps HR teams identify risk factors contributing to employee turnover and supports data-driven retention strategies.

---

##  Objective
To monitor employee attrition trends, analyze demographic impacts on turnover, and assess job satisfaction distribution by role, department, and age group.

---

##  Key KPIs & Metrics

- **Total Employee Count**
- **Attrition Count & Attrition Rate (%)**
- **Active Employees**
- **Average Employee Age**
- **Attrition by Department**
- **Attrition by Age Group**
- **Attrition by Gender**
- **Education Field-wise Attrition**
- **Job Satisfaction by Role**
- **Employee Distribution by Age Group**

---

##  Visual Insights

- **Pie Chart:** Attrition by Department  
- **Histogram:** Employee Count by Age Group  
- **Bar Chart:** Education Field-wise Attrition  
- **Donut Charts:** Attrition Rate by Age (with Gender Split)  
- **Matrix Table:** Job Satisfaction by Role  
- **KPI Cards:** Overall Attrition Metrics & Gender Split  

---

##  Data Source

- **HR Dataset (1,470 rows, 42 columns)**
  - Fields include: `Attrition`, `Age`, `Gender`, `Job Role`, `Department`, `Education`, `Distance From Home`, `Over Time`, `Job Satisfaction`, `Monthly Income`, `Attrition Count`, `Attrition Rate`
