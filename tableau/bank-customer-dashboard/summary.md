#  UK Bank Customer Segmentation Dashboard 

## 📌 Overview
The **UK Bank Customer Segmentation Dashboard** provides an interactive analysis of customer demographics, regional distribution, account balance distribution, and job classification breakdown.  
This dashboard supports the bank's strategy in understanding customer profiles across the UK to guide marketing, product offerings, and customer relationship management.

---

##  Business Objective
To segment the bank’s customer base by demographics, job classification, region, and financial behavior to identify patterns that inform business strategy and customer engagement initiatives.

---

##  Key KPIs & Metrics Tracked
- Total Customer Count
- Balance Distribution
- Age Group Distribution
- Customer Segmentation by Region
- Job Classification Distribution
- Gender Split of Customers

---

##  Visuals & Insights Provided

- **Map Visualization:** Customer distribution by region with total counts.
- **Bar Chart:** Balance Distribution by customer percentage.
- **Treemap:** Job Classification segmentation (White Collar, Blue Collar, Other).
- **Histogram:** Age Distribution of customers.
- **Pie Chart:** Gender Split across the customer base.
- **Dynamic Filters:** Age Groups, Balance Groups.

---

##  Dashboard Insights Summary

### Customer Concentration by Region
- **England** holds the highest customer base (**2,159 customers**).
- **Scotland** follows with **1,124 customers**.
- **Wales** and **Northern Ireland** have smaller segments with **520** and **211** customers respectively.

---

### Balance Distribution
- The majority of customers have balances between **0 - 36K**.
- A significant drop-off is observed in customers with balances above **72K**.
- Indicates a customer base skewed towards lower balance brackets.

---

### Job Classification Trends
- **White Collar workers** make up the largest segment (~70%).
- **Blue Collar** and **Other** categories form the remaining share.
- Suggests the bank primarily serves professional-class customers.

---

### Age Distribution Patterns
- Customers aged **30-40 years** form the largest age group.
- A smaller proportion of customers are below **20** or above **50**.
- The bank’s customer base is largely mid-aged adults.

---

### Gender Split
- **Male customers (54%)** slightly outnumber **Female customers (46%)**.
- Gender distribution is relatively balanced, with a slight male dominance.
