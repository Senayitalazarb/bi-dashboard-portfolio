#  Customer Analysis Dashboard

## 📌 Overview
This Tableau dashboard visualizes customer behavior and revenue performance across product categories, regions, customer segments, and time. It provides an interactive view into how revenue is generated by demographics and purchasing patterns, helping businesses make data-driven marketing and sales decisions.

---

##  Objective
To identify revenue-driving customer segments, understand regional trends, and analyze the relationship between discounting, quantity, and purchase behavior.

---

##  Key KPIs & Visuals

- **Month-wise Revenue**  
  → Line chart showing revenue trends from Oct 2020 to Jul 2021.  
  → Peak observed in **January 2021 ($57.6M)**.

- **State-wise Revenue (Map)**  
  → US map shaded by total revenue per state.  
  → Highest in **CA, TX, NY**.

- **Age-wise Revenue**  
  → Bar chart showing age group contribution.  
  → Leading group: **30–40 ($44.8M)**.

- **Quantity vs. Discount Scatter Plot**  
  → Identifies correlation between discount % and quantity ordered.

- **Region-wise Revenue Share**  
  → Pie chart showing contribution by region.  
  → **South: 38.4%**, **Midwest: 26.9%**.

- **Gender-wise Revenue by Category**  
  → Shows gender breakdown across top categories like **Mobiles**, **Appliances**, and **Fashion**.

---

##  Insights

- The **South region** dominates total revenue with nearly 40% share.
- The **30–40 age group** is the most valuable demographic.
- A significant **revenue spike occurred in January 2021**.
- Discounting does not always correlate with higher quantity purchased.
- Gender-based preferences are distinct: for example, **Fashion skews female**, while **Mobiles and Appliances** are more evenly split.

---

##  Filters / Interactions

- **Category Filter**: Applies across all visuals for focused analysis.
- **Hover Interactions**: Reveal state-level revenue and demographic splits.
- **Legend Selections**: Let users isolate regions, genders, or age brackets.

---

## 📁 Data Source

- Dataset: `sales_06_FY2020-21.csv`
- Fields include: `Order Date`, `Region`, `State`, `Category`, `Qty Ordered`, `Total`, `Age (bin)`, `Gender`, `Discount Percent`, etc.
- Source represents ~10,000 records across FY 2020–21.

---

## 🛠️ Tools & Techniques

- **Tableau Desktop** for dashboard development
- **Bar, Line, Pie, and Map Charts** for visual analysis
- **Scatter Plot & Filters** to analyze quantity vs. discount
- **Calculated Fields** to segment age groups, discounts, and regions
- **Custom Bins** for age and discount grouping
