#  Customer Analysis – Tableau Dashboard

## 🎯 Purpose
To visualize revenue trends across time, geography, demographics, and product segments to identify key drivers of sales performance.

## 📈 Key Visuals

- **Month-wise Revenue**  
  → Highlights monthly sales trends.  
  → Peak: **January 2021 ($57.6M)**.

- **State-wise Revenue (Map)**  
  → US map colored by total revenue.  
  → Top contributors: **CA, TX, NY**.

- **Age-wise Revenue**  
  → Shows revenue by customer age group.  
  → Highest: **30–40 ($44.8M)**.

- **Quantity vs. Discount Correlation**  
  → Scatter plot of discount % vs. quantity ordered.  
  → Mixed correlation patterns.

- **Region-wise Revenue Share (%)**  
  → Donut chart by region.  
  → Top: **South (38.4%)**, Midwest (26.9%).

- **Gender-wise Revenue**  
  → Revenue comparison by gender and category.  
  → Notable: **Mobiles**, **Appliances**, **Fashion**.

## 💡 Insights

- **South** region dominates revenue share.  
- **30–40** age group leads all segments.  
- Revenue spikes notably in **Jan 2021**.  
- **Discounts** do not always result in higher order volumes.  
- Gender-based category preference is visible (e.g., Mobiles high for both, Fashion varies).

## 🔍 Filters
- **Category Selector**: Dynamically filters all visuals.
